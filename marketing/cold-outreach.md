# Cold Outreach Email Templates

## How to Use
1. Find targets on LinkedIn (CTOs, VP Engineering, IT Directors, DevOps leads)
2. Use their work email (hunter.io, apollo.io, or LinkedIn direct message)
3. Personalize the [BRACKETS] sections
4. Send 10-20 per day max to avoid spam filters
5. Follow up once after 3-4 days if no response
